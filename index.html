ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Mateo Suffern, a Master's student in Mathematical-Computational Modeling and Data Science at the University of Buenos Aires. My main research interest is geometry, supported by coursework in geometric measure theory, stochastic geometry, complex networks, and topological data analysis. Currently, I'm preparing my Master's thesis on mathematical statistical physics.
